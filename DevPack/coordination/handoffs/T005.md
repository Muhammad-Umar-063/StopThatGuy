**Behavior implemented**
- New workspace package `@stopthatguy/world` exports `FIRST_STREET_WORLD` (`first-street-1`), `FIRST_STREET_IDS` and `AUTHORED_WORLDS`. The data is frozen and follows the shared `WorldData` contract and the geometry.ts convention.
- The world is a blockout of an adapted Baixa street:
  - a 60 m three-lane street from the bank square (dead end with U-turn) to a T-junction;
  - a three-lane hill street climbing 3 m over 60 m (5 %) to a hill-top U-turn;
  - a single-lane alley (foot and bicycle) with a U-turn;
  - a foot-only stair-and-terrace shortcut (75.6 m, against 100.1 m by road; 37.5 % stair grade);
  - 8 directed edges, 8 junctions with turn windows and turn paths, 7 temporary collision proxies (bank, blocks, bike stand, lamp) and one camera proxy;
  - spawns for 6 players, the robber and 2 public bicycles.
- There are no exits or escape routes yet, so validation reports one documented warning.
- `validateWorldData` gained a layout phase. It runs only when structure and references are valid, and checks:
  - foot reachability from every player spawn (`disconnectedStart`) and reachable exits (`unreachableExit`);
  - per-mode continuation, so no mode is stranded (`missingTurnaround`);
  - spawn/collider overlap (`spawnInsideCollider`) and spawn/spawn overlap;
  - grade limits: 15 % wheeled (`illegalVehicleRoute`) and 80 % foot;
  - turn-path ends on the ground (within 0.3 m).
- New helpers `pointOnEdge`, `spawnPosition`, `reachableEdges` and `WORLD_LAYOUT_LIMITS` support it. The limits are provisional, assistant-selected values. Eight new invalid samples each produce only their intended code.
- `npm run validate` now checks every authored world, and the root build order is shared → world → simulation → apps.
