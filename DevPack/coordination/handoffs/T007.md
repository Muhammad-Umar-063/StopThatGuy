**Behavior implemented** (`apps/client/src/input/keyboard/`)
- Keys are matched by `KeyboardEvent.code` and mapped to shared intents: ArrowUp/ArrowDown/Shift drive `forwardHeld`/`brakeHeld`/`sprintHeld`. ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, F and X send `left`/`right`/`capture`/`sabotage` edges. E sends one `interact` edge per press and also drives `interactHeld`. Escape emits a local `{ type: "toggleMenu" }` intent, which is never sent to the server.
- Edges are sent once per fresh press. Key-repeat, and a keydown without a keyup, add nothing. Key-repeat never sets a held control.
- Up and Down held together are both reported; `brakeHeld` wins in the simulation, per the contract.
- Left and right pressed within one sample cancel each other. A rollover press is a new request.
- Action IDs are assigned at `sample()` from an injected session-scoped source (`createActionIdSequence`), which is uint32-checked and strictly increasing. At most 8 presses are queued between samples.
- Gameplay takes a key only when it is active (default off) and the key has no Ctrl/Alt/Meta modifier or IME composition. Focus must also not be in an input, textarea, select, contenteditable or key-handling ARIA widget, and must be inside the optional `surface`. Only then is the default prevented.
- Releases are always processed. Everything is released, and unsent presses are dropped, on window blur, hidden visibility, `pagehide`, focus moving off gameplay, `setGameplayActive(false)`, `releaseAll()` and Meta keyup.
- `dispose()` is idempotent and removes every listener. Re-attaching to the same window disposes the previous controller.

**Browser evidence** (real trusted CDP key events)
- Arrows and their repeats don't scroll while gameplay owns focus. They do scroll when inactive, after dispose, or on a button outside the surface.
- Chrome sets `event.repeat`. A text field keeps typing, caret arrows and Escape. Tab/Shift+Tab focus navigation works.
- A real tab switch (blur followed by hidden) releases controls. Returning plus auto-repeat restores nothing.
- `DOMDebugger.getEventListeners` shows exactly one listener set after three attaches, and none after dispose.

**Not run / limitations**
- Not tested in Firefox, Safari or Edge, on Windows or macOS, or on real hardware. The macOS lost-keyup handling is unverified.
- In Chrome, blur and hidden fire together. Blur-only and hidden-only release are covered only by unit tests with fakes.
- Bindings use QWERTY key positions, with no rebinding.
